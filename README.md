# Hiba Sofa Showcase

Prompt for Lovable AI:

Build a premium catalog/showcase website (not a shopping-cart e-commerce site) for "Hiba Sofa Works," a custom sofa manufacturing, reupholstery, and repair brand based in Bengaluru, India, established in 2016, offering pan-India delivery.

Brand Slogan: "Design with Dignity" — use this prominently under the logo in the header/hero, and in the footer.

Business Details:

Name: Hiba Sofa Works

Slogan: Design with Dignity

Established: 2016

Address: Veeranna Layout, 12/24 K, Kodigehalli - Thindlu Main Rd, GD Layout, Doddabommasandra, Vidyaranyapura, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560097

Phone/WhatsApp: 070192 75831

Hours: 9:30 AM – 9:30 PM, daily

Google rating: 4.6 (75+ reviews) — display as a trust badge

Brand & Visual Identity:

Use the attached logo (white line-art sofa icon with "HIBA SOFA WORKS" wordmark). Also generate a simplified icon-only version (just the sofa line-art) for favicon and mobile header, plus a dark-on-light variant of the full logo for use on light backgrounds/footer.

Color palette: warm espresso brown (#3B2A20) and cream/ivory (#F5EFE6) as primary tones, with a deep terracotta or muted olive green (#8A5A44 / #5C6B4F) as accent for CTAs — evokes upholstery fabric and craftsmanship, not a generic tech e-commerce look.

Typography: clean modern sans-serif for body (Inter or Poppins), paired with an elegant serif or the logo's lettering style for headings.

Tone: warm, artisanal, trustworthy — boutique workshop feel.

Lead-Generation Model (no payment/checkout):

No cart, no online payment. Every product and service page drives toward: floating WhatsApp button (bottom-right, links to 070192 75831), "Call Now" button, and a simple inquiry/quote form (name, phone, city, product interest, room dimensions if relevant, message).

Forms should feel low-friction — this is the primary conversion path, so make CTAs prominent throughout, not just on one page.

Site Structure & Pages:

Home Page

Hero with a real lifestyle photo of a finished sofa, slogan "Design with Dignity" beneath the logo, headline about custom sofas made to fit your space, CTA ("Get a Custom Quote" / "WhatsApp Us")

Trust bar: "Trusted Since 2016 · 4.6★ (75+ Reviews) · Pan-India Delivery"

"Why Hiba Sofa Works": custom sizing to room dimensions, expert reupholstery & repair, quality craftsmanship, nearly a decade of experience

Featured product carousel (real photos)

Before/after transformation gallery (real repair/rework photos, side-by-side slider if possible)

Testimonials pulling from real Google reviews (customized cot, sofa set, reupholstered recliner reviews)

Footer: logo + slogan, address, phone, hours, social links, service area (Pan-India delivery), "Since 2016"

Shop / Catalog Page

Product grid filtered by category: 3-Seater, Corner Sofa, 3+1+1 Set, 3+2 Set, Lounge Sofa

Each card: real photo, name, starting price (optional, or "Price on request"), "Enquire / Customize" button

Filters: seating capacity, fabric type, price range (if shown)

Product Detail Page

Image gallery of real photos (multiple angles/fabric options)

Description, dimensions, starting price or "request quote"

Customization inquiry form: room dimensions, fabric/color preference, seating configuration, notes

Care/warranty info section

Reupholstery & Repair Services Page

Explain the transformation service, reinforced by "Design with Dignity" — giving old furniture new life and dignity

Before/after gallery using real photos

Inquiry form with photo upload of the customer's existing sofa, issue description, contact details

About Us Page

Brand story since 2016, craftsmanship philosophy, what "Design with Dignity" means to the brand, workshop/team photos

Contact Page

Full address with embedded Google Map, phone, WhatsApp button, hours (9:30 AM–9:30 PM daily), contact form

Functionality:

Mobile-responsive (most furniture buyers browse on mobile)

Real customer reviews/ratings displayed on relevant pages

Simple content-manageable product catalog (use Supabase via Lovable if backend needed, to store products and inquiry submissions)

All forms route to a visible email/WhatsApp notification (or store in Supabase for you to review)

Key Messaging Throughout:

"Design with Dignity" — quality craftsmanship and respectful, personal customer care

Customization to exact room size — not one-size-fits-all

Nearly a decade of trusted expertise (since 2016) in sofa repair and transformation, not just new sales

Quality that justifies the price — build trust through visible material/craft details (fabric quality, foam density, frame material) and real before/after proof website
